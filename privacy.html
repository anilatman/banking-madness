Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Summary

Banking Madness does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the game.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Game progress

Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Device permissions

The game does not use your camera, microphone, photos or location, and it never asks for access to them.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. It works fully offline.

In-game currency

Coins and gems are earned only by playing. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
